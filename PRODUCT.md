# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS via Jekyll, GitHub Pages native build (no framework, no
JS toolchain, no CI to write — GitHub Pages builds Jekyll automatically).
Decided during design brainstorm, recorded here rather than asked again.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers at French AI/ML companies evaluating Arnaud
Thery for AI/ML engineer roles, arriving via a link shared in job
applications or on LinkedIn. Secondary: Arnaud himself, sharing the link as
his public professional presence.

## Product Purpose

A public complement to the PDF CV generated by Diggo (his job-search
pipeline) — a fast, always-available overview for a recruiter who won't
open a PDF, plus a portfolio section with more project depth than a CV
allows. Success: a recruiter gets a credible, scannable impression in
under a minute and can go deeper (CV, projects, GitHub) if interested.

## Positioning

Senior on relational maturity, still building on the technical stack: 8
years managing a team (Fnac, department manager) — communication,
prioritization, pressure management — combined with 2+ years of hands-on
production AI/ML engineering (Missia). The site should give the management
experience real weight, not bury it under a skills-tag list, and pair it
with concrete technical proof (shipped systems, not just tools used).

## Operating Context

Shared as a link in job applications and on LinkedIn, alongside the
Diggo-generated PDF CV and cover letter — not a replacement for either.
Recruiters skim in seconds before deciding whether to open the CV page or
click through to a project's GitHub repo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static content only — no backend, no contact form with server-side
  processing, no CMS.
- Bilingual FR/EN, both languages fully written (not a translated
  afterthought) — content rewritten once for the web from
  `diggo/config/cv.yaml`, then maintained independently (no sync pipeline).
- No custom domain for v1 (`github.io` subdomain).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Arnaud Thery. No existing personal logo, visual identity, or stated
voice — first personal site.

## Product Principles

- Credibility over hype: every claim backed by something checkable (a
  GitHub link, a live demo, a merged PR) — no unverifiable superlatives.
- The management years are a feature, not a footnote: give them real
  narrative space, not a buried bullet under a technical skills list.
- Scannable first, detailed second: the homepage must land the value
  proposition before any scroll; depth lives on the CV/Projects pages.
- Bilingual parity: FR and EN pages are equally complete.
- Low maintenance: fully static, no infrastructure to babysit during an
  active job search.
